About Me

Hi, I'm Franciszek. I live in Helsinki and I'm intersted in electronics and coding.

I write firmware for Arduino boards, wire up sensors and actuators, design parts in CAD and bring them to life on 3D printer. Then I connect everything with software - scripts, dashboards and small web tools - so that devices can talk to each other (hello, IoT).

My loop is simple: idea → prototype → print → flash → break → fix. Right now I'm ready for the Junction hackathon.
